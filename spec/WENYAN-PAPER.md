# Wenyan v1.0 Whitepaper Draft

## Abstract
Wenyan models typed governance messages as a sealed, append-only system with deterministic replay.

## 1. Categorical Semantics
Documents inhabit a typed schema layer (Ti), transitions compose through pipeline stages, and seals witness each morphism.

## 2. Constitutional Safety
Constitutional updates are gated by consensus thresholds and archive-backed law resolution.

## 3. Merkle DAG Soundness
Archive state roots are deterministic over immutable transition history and audit leaves.

## Appendix
Operational naming follows historical documentary terminology as a mnemonic, while runtime rules remain machine-verifiable.
